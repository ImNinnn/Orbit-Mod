Terms of Service

Orbit is a free, community-made Minecraft mod. These terms are the rules for using it and Orbit Cloud, the online service that shares cosmetics and emotes between players and carries friend requests and messages. Orbit asks you to accept them, and the Privacy Policy, the first time you start the game with it. If you don't accept, Orbit doesn't connect to Orbit Cloud.

What these terms cover

They cover the Orbit mod, Orbit Cloud (api.orbitcosmetics.dev), and the Orbit server mod and plugin. The mod's source code is public on GitHub.

Orbit is not an official Minecraft product. It is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. You still have to follow the Minecraft EULA and the rules of any server you play on.

Your account

Orbit Cloud knows you by your Minecraft account. You sign in through Minecraft's own sign-in check, so Orbit never sees your password or your Microsoft account. You're responsible for what's done with your account.

What you share

When you play on a server, the cosmetics and emotes you wear are uploaded to Orbit Cloud, and other Orbit players there can see them. You keep the rights to what you made. By uploading it, you allow Orbit Cloud to store it and show it to other players, for as long as you share it.

Only share what you made yourself or are allowed to share. Featured catalog items belong to their creators; you can wear them, but not sell or republish them as your own.

Messages between friends are end-to-end encrypted when you turn it on for a conversation (the lock); then Orbit Cloud can't read them. You're responsible for what you send, and for what you write on your profile: your description, pronouns and outfit. Friends can report a profile, and moderators can act on it like on anything else.

Rules

  • No hateful, sexual, violent or otherwise offensive cosmetics, names or messages.
  • No harassment, threats, spam, or messages sent to players who blocked you through another account.
  • Don't impersonate other players, Mojang, or Orbit's moderators.
  • Don't upload malware, or files made to crash or slow down other players' games.
  • Don't attack, overload or scrape Orbit Cloud, or try to get around its limits, bans or encryption.
  • Don't use Orbit to break the law or a server's rules.

Moderation

Players can report others from the game. Moderators may then block a cosmetic, or ban a player from sharing anything except featured items, or from Orbit Cloud entirely, for a while or for good. A banned player still sees other players' cosmetics. Moderators can't read messages, but they can act on what is reported to them.

Hosting worlds

Orbit can put your world online through e4mc, a separate mod and service with its own terms. Anyone with the address can try to join a hosted world, so only share it with people you trust. You're responsible for your world and who you let in.

The service

Orbit and Orbit Cloud are provided free and as they are, without any warranty. Orbit Cloud may be slow, go offline, lose data, change or end at any time, and some features are still in beta. Keep your own copies of anything you make: the files in your Orbit folder stay on your computer.

As far as the law allows, the people who make Orbit aren't liable for any loss or damage from using it, or from not being able to use it.

You can stop using Orbit Cloud at any time: turn it off in Orbit's settings, or remove the mod.

Changes

These terms may change as Orbit does. When they change, the date at the top changes too, and Orbit asks you to accept the new version the next time you start the game.

Contact

Questions about these terms: open an issue on GitHub. To report a player, use Report in the game.
